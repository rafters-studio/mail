`@rafters/mail/send`: a `Written` goes to a submission server as its exact bytes. `createSender({ transport, sends })` records each envelope recipient as unknown before anything leaves, sends only to recipients with no row or a row refused and not final, and answers one `Outcome` per recipient. `smtpTransport`, `submit` and `stuff` speak the session; `streamConnection` turns a byte stream into lines under one timeout; `sendEventSchema` is the one shape of what a provider reports afterwards.
